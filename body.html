Take a job request and track it

One customer request becomes a priority, a checklist, a message, a calendar event and a spreadsheet row.

Nothing leaves this browser. Requests are saved only on this device, the page's security settings block it from sending data anywhere, and it isn't connected to any calendar, email or text service. Please use made-up details, not real ones.

How it works

A customer fills in one form. The page checks every field, sets a priority by rules you can read below the form, and produces a job checklist, a message to send the customer, a calendar event and a spreadsheet row. The office then moves each job along a status board: New, Scheduled, In progress and Done. "Load sample data" puts 8 made-up jobs on the board.

    How priority is set

    The first rule that fits decides it.

    1. Urgent if water is leaking right now.
    2. Urgent if sewage is backing up.
    3. Urgent if there's no heat and the request comes in between October and April.
    4. Soon if the preferred visit is within 7 days.
    5. Routine for everything else.

    A gas smell never becomes a booking. The form shows safety steps instead.

    What this would look like for your business

    This demo stops at the point where tools usually stop talking to each other: it makes the calendar file, the message and the spreadsheet row, and you move them yourself. A version built for you could send the confirmation by email or text, put the visit in your real calendar and add the row to your spreadsheet or job software, with your own request types, rules and checklists.
